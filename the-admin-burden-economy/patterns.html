The synthesis · what repeats across all 13

The patterns

Line up every corner of the paperwork economy — the billers, the outsourcers, the denial-fighters, the automation vendors — and the same handful of mechanics repeat, all flowing from one fact: the friction is somebody's revenue.

Each page asks: what did the admin-burden economy make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly valuable? Thirteen answers later, the same tensions repeat.

The one thing to take away

American life and business run on staggering amounts of paperwork, and a whole economy grew up on both sides of it — the firms that process the friction and the firms that promise to tame it. The catch is the perverse alignment at its heart: the friction is somebody's revenue, so nobody in the machine is fully on the side of making the paperwork disappear. Healthcare is the flagship, where roughly a quarter of every dollar goes to admin — waste to the system, but a Fortune-10-sized business to the middle.
