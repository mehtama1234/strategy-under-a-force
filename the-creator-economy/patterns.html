The synthesis · what repeats across all 13

The patterns

Line up every corner of the creator economy — the creators, the tools, the platforms, the superfans, the AI — and the same handful of mechanics repeat, all flowing from one fact: attention became income.

Each page asks: what did the creator economy make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly valuable? Thirteen answers later, the same tensions repeat.

The one thing to take away

The internet turned attention into income, so the influencer became a media company of one. But the reliable money is selling shovels to creators and owning the audience — not being a creator. The platforms take a cut of everything, the tools charge monthly, the agencies broker the deals, and the few creators who win are the ones who own the product and the relationship instead of renting them from an algorithm. Meanwhile the vast majority earn almost nothing — the most "democratized" economy turned out to be the most unequal.
