The synthesis · what repeats across all 13

The patterns

Line up what the index revolution did, and one theme dominates: a great deal for savers quietly concentrated ownership and power in three firms.

Each page asks: what did passive investing make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly valuable? Thirteen answers later, the same tensions repeat.

The one thing to take away

Passive investing was a genuine gift to ordinary savers — cheap, diversified, better after fees. But because everyone bought the same funds, three firms became the biggest owner of nearly every company at once, and a product designed to be neutral and boring quietly concentrated ownership, votes, and market power.
