Group B · The store becomes a media network

The physical screens — every surface is becoming an ad

The gas pump, the checkout, the elevator, the billboard. The real world is being turned into ad inventory.

The last place without targeted ads was the physical world — so it's being retrofitted with screens. Gas pumps, checkout aisles, elevators, taxi tops, and billboards are being networked into a real-time ad market that can be targeted and measured using your phone's location data. It's called "digital out of home" (DOOH), and it's booming.

Screens everywhere

  • Gas pumps: GSTV plays video ads at ~1,500+ locations, reaching 100M+ drivers a month, targeted with fuel-purchase data.
  • In-store: Walmart and others fill aisles and self-checkout with ad screens (a $2–3B sub-segment growing 15%+).
  • Elevators & gyms: Captivate runs ~6,000+ elevator and ~800+ fitness-center screens.
  • Billboards: Lamar, Outfront, and Clear Channel now auction ~30–40% of digital-billboard space programmatically, using foot-traffic data.

The main worry

The single biggest risk — surveillance with no opt-out

Unlike online ads, you can't opt out of the physical world. DOOH runs on location tracking — geofencing, foot-traffic attribution, mobile IDs — that follows you into the real world with no disclosure or consent. "Anonymized" location data is often re-identifiable, and the rules haven't caught up.

The surprising part

There's genuinely no escaping it offline anymore. You can't refuel, ride an elevator, or check out without meeting a targeted ad — and the ad knows what neighborhood you're in and which competitor's store you visited last week. And the businesses want more of it: gas stations and retailers take 50–70% of the ad revenue on screens in their space, so they're actively installing more. Every surface is being turned into inventory.

In one sentence

The physical screens in a nutshell

Every physical surface — pump, shelf, elevator, billboard — is being networked into a real-time, location-targeted ad market that follows you from online into the real world, with no off switch.
