Everyone's an Ad Business

How companies discovered that selling ads to their own audience is the best business they have.

The eleventh force — a close cousin of the attention economy and the loyalty economy. Advertising is near-pure profit: if you already have an audience — shoppers, riders, viewers, a screen, or data — selling an ad against it costs almost nothing. So the one idea that ties it together: once you have an audience, the ads are almost free money — and everyone became an ad seller.

The one question, asked of everyone

What did the "everything is an ad business" shift make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly worth money for this company? The "real" product — the ride, the groceries, the TV, the bank account — increasingly exists to assemble the audience the ads are sold against. More and more, the true product is you.
