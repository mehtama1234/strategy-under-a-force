# The Ozempic Effect

A plain-English collection: **how weight-loss / GLP-1 drugs (Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound) are reshaping company strategy** across many industries.

Sister project to `company-strategy-under-ai` — the *same method, a different force.* Where that one asked "what did AI change for this company?", this one asks the same of the weight-loss-drug boom.

Date baseline: 2026-08-06

## The one question, asked of everyone

> What did the weight-loss-drug boom make **scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly valuable** for this company?

## Groups

- **A. The makers** — Novo Nordisk · Eli Lilly · the next-pill race · the sellers/copycats
- **B. The ones getting eaten** — Big Food & snacks · soda & alcohol · WeightWatchers
- **C. Adapting & selling to the trend** — pharmacies & PBMs · "GLP-1 friendly" food · protein/supplements
- **D. Surprising medical ripples** — sleep apnea · diabetes devices · dialysis/kidneys
- **E. The money & the country** — Novo Holdings & Denmark · who pays

## Method

Research run on **Haiku** (cheap, parallel); pages written in the collection's plain-English house style; verified facts kept separate from marketing/analyst hype. Open `index.html` for the grouped hub.
