# The Ozempic Effect — Goal

Date baseline: 2026-08-06

## Objective

A plain-English collection analyzing **how weight-loss / GLP-1 drugs (Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound) are reshaping company strategy** across many industries. Sister project to `company-strategy-under-ai` — the *same method, a different force.*

## The method (reused)

```
1. Pick one big force (here: GLP-1 / weight-loss drugs).
2. Ask every company the same question: what did this force make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly valuable for you?
3. Group companies by the KIND of answer (archetypes).
4. Research each — plain English, verified facts kept separate from hype.
5. Pull out the patterns that repeat.
```

## Why GLP-1 is the perfect second subject

Like AI, it's *one force reshaping many unrelated industries at once* — drugmakers, food, drinks, pharmacies, medical devices, even a national economy (Denmark). Most people haven't connected the dots.

## Groups (archetypes)

- **A. The makers** — Novo Nordisk, Eli Lilly, the next-pill race, the sellers/copycats.
- **B. The ones getting eaten** — Big Food & snacks, soda & alcohol, WeightWatchers.
- **C. Adapting & selling to the trend** — pharmacies & PBMs, "GLP-1 friendly" food, protein/supplements.
- **D. Surprising medical ripples** — sleep apnea, diabetes devices, dialysis/kidneys.
- **E. The money & the country** — Novo Holdings & Denmark, who pays (insurers/Medicare).

## Method note

Research is run on **Haiku** (bulk, parallel, cost-efficient); pages are written in the collection's plain-English house style. Verified facts are separated from marketing/analyst hype; forward-looking numbers flagged.
