Denmark — one drug that moved a whole country

Novo Nordisk got so big it single-handedly held up a national economy — then dragged it back down.

This is the wildest ripple of all: a weight-loss drug reshaping not a company or an industry, but an entire country. Novo Nordisk grew so large that it propped up Denmark's whole economy — its growth, its exports, even its stock market.

How one company carried a nation

The number that says it all

In 2023, Denmark's economy grew 1.8% while much of Europe stagnated. But strip out the drug industry and Denmark's economy would have actually shrunk — Novo and pharma supplied roughly half of the country's growth. For a couple of years, Denmark grew two to four times faster than the rest of Europe, almost entirely because of one company.

bigger
At its 2024 peak, Novo Nordisk was worth more than Denmark's entire yearly economic output — and made up about 40% of the country's goods exports.

A charity steering a national champion

Novo Nordisk is controlled by a charitable foundation (Novo Holdings), which reinvests the company's wealth. It made a huge move — buying a big drug-manufacturing company (Catalent, ~$16.5 billion) — to help pump out more Ozempic. So a foundation quietly ended up steering the fortunes of a whole nation.

The twist: the crash cut the other way

The "one company" risk, made real

When you lean this hard on a single company, its bad year becomes the country's bad year. In 2025–26 Novo lost the race to Eli Lilly, its value crashed ~75%, and it cut about 9,000 jobs (over half in Denmark). Denmark's central bank slashed its growth forecast (from 3.6% down to 2.0%). The concentration that lifted the country up now pulled it back — exactly the "one big company" risk economists had warned about (think Nokia and Finland).

The surprising part

A single class of weight-loss drugs moved an entire wealthy nation's economy up — and then down. It's rare for one company to matter this much to a rich country; usually that only happens with oil states. Denmark's leaders publicly waved off the worry, but the 2025 stumble showed how real it was: when the country's champion sneezes, the whole economy catches a cold.

In one sentence

Denmark in a nutshell

Novo Nordisk's weight-loss boom carried Denmark's entire economy for two years — proof of how big this drug got — and its stumble dragged the country down, proving the danger of leaning on one company.
