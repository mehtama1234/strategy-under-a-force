Group B · The silicon & the toll

The GPU chokehold

One company's chips — and its software — sit under the entire AI industry, taxing all of it.

The specialized chips that train and run AI are dominated by Nvidia, whose CUDA software lock-in and supply control give it roughly 80–90% of the AI-chip market and, at times, the highest market cap on earth. The whole build-out runs through one company's silicon — and, more importantly, one company's software.

The core bet

Control the one scarce input everyone needs — the AI chip — plus the software everyone builds on, and you tax the entire industry every time it grows.

What the numbers say

~$4T
Nvidia's market cap in 2025–26, briefly the world's most valuable company (from ~$100B in 2017).
80–92%
Nvidia's share of the AI-accelerator market — over 90% for training chips.
$15B → $115B
Data-center revenue growth from fiscal 2023 to fiscal 2025 — a staggering ramp.
~75%
Gross margin — Nvidia keeps three-quarters of every dollar of revenue.
19 yrs
How long the CUDA software ecosystem has been compounding — the real lock-in.
MI300 / TPU
AMD's chip and Google's TPU are the main challengers — mostly software-layer attacks on the moat.

The software is the moat, not the chip

Rivals like AMD's MI300X can beat Nvidia on paper and cost 30–50% less, yet customers still buy Nvidia because CUDA — 19 years of libraries, tools, and trained engineers — is too deep to cross. Nvidia sells the shovels while everyone else digs for uncertain gold, and the shovels only work with Nvidia's handle.

The single biggest worry

The biggest customers — Google, Meta, Amazon — are designing their own chips to escape the toll; if enough succeed, the lock loosens. Export controls to China and a sudden demand air-pocket could each hit those 75% margins fast.

The surprising part

The revealing detail is that the hardware advantage is almost beside the point. Competitors have shipped faster, cheaper chips and still lost, because the switching cost isn't the silicon — it's rewriting a decade of software and retraining every engineer. Nvidia's real product is the ecosystem everyone is already standing in.

In one sentence

Nvidia became the toll-taker of AI by locking the industry into its chips and its software, so every AI company must pay its prices or spend years escaping.
