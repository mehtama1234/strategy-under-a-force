The Remote Shift

How work leaving the office reshaped company strategy.

The ninth force, same method as the eight before it. For a century, work meant going to a place — an office downtown, five days a week. Remote and hybrid work broke that. And the load-bearing thing that got disrupted wasn't the office; it was the commute. The one idea that ties it together: work stopped being a place, and the whole economy built around the daily commute unravelled.

The one question, asked of everyone

What did the remote shift make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly worth money for this company? When the commute stopped, value moved from the expensive center (downtown) to the cheaper edges (the home, the suburbs, anywhere). Big caveat: only ~a third of jobs can go remote — so flexibility became a class divide too.
