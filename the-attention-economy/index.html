The Attention Economy

How the fight for your eyes and minutes reshaped company strategy.

The eighth force, same method as the seven before it. Making content and connection became nearly free and infinite — so the one thing that can't grow is your attention (24 hours a day, a fixed set of eyeballs). The whole economy reorganizes around capturing it. The one idea that ties it together: attention is the last scarce resource, and everything now competes for the same minutes.

The one question, asked of everyone

What did the attention economy make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly worth money for this company? Because content is infinite, the power shifts from making it to surfacing it — so the algorithm beats the content. And every minute is zero-sum: the fight isn't for market share, it's for minutes.
