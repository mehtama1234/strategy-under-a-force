The Gambling Economy

How betting escaped the casino and moved into every screen you own.

The 24th force. Gambling used to require going somewhere — Vegas, a lottery counter. Now the casino is in your pocket, open 24/7, nudging you with free bets and live odds. Legalization (US sports betting since 2018) plus the smartphone turned betting into a one-tap habit — and the same slot-machine psychology leaked into trading apps, video games, and "prediction markets." The one idea: every screen became a casino.

The one question, asked of everyone

What did the gambling economy make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly worth money? Value flows to whoever owns the betting screen and the odds — while sports leagues, media, banks, and states all take a cut and got hooked on the revenue. The risk: engineered addiction and financial harm, paid mostly by the heaviest users.
