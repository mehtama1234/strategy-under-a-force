# The Gambling Economy — strategy under a force

The 24th collection.

**The force:** gambling went from a walled-off vice (Vegas, a lottery ticket) to something embedded in every screen — sports apps, trading apps, video games, prediction markets. Legalization (US sports betting since 2018) plus the smartphone turned betting into an always-on, one-tap habit, and a widening set of "not-technically-gambling" products (loot boxes, gamified stock/crypto trading, prediction markets) blur the line. We ask: *what did the gambling economy make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly valuable?*

**The signature insight (plain English):** every screen became a casino. Betting used to require going somewhere; now the casino is in your pocket, open 24/7, nudging you with free bets and live odds. Sports leagues, media, banks, and app stores all take a cut, and states got hooked on the tax revenue. Meanwhile the same slot-machine psychology leaks into trading apps, video games, and "prediction markets" that are gambling in all but name. Value flows to whoever owns the betting screen and the odds; the risk is a rise in addiction and financial harm, paid mostly by the heaviest users.
