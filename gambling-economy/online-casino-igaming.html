Group A · The legalization gold rush

Online casino / iGaming

The hard drug of the sector: 24/7 slots in your pocket.

Beyond sports betting, a handful of states legalized full online casino gambling — slots, blackjack, live dealer — which is far more lucrative and more addictive per user. It's available 24/7, needs no event, and keeps players solitary and fast: the perfect addiction machine. Operators lobby hardest to expand it, and it's already outrunning sports betting where it's legal.

The core bet

The bet: online casino extracts far more lifetime value per user than sports betting, from fewer people. It's event-free (play anytime), fast (sub-3-second slot cycles), and solitary — the highest-margin, highest-addiction product in gambling. That's exactly why operators push for it and regulators fear it.

Why it's the "hard drug"

A sports bet is event-based — you wait for the game. A slot is endless: tap, result, tap, result, all night, alone. That solitary, instant, no-limit design correlates with much higher problem-gambling rates, especially among young men (a ~10% problem rate in 18–30 men). It's cannibalizing physical casinos and capturing younger, lonelier audiences.

The single biggest worry

iGaming's 24/7, solitary, instant-feedback model extracts far higher lifetime value from fewer, more vulnerable people — and operators lobby to bring it to all 50 states precisely because a small fraction of addicted players fund the whole profit pool.

The surprising part

Online casino surpassed land-based casino revenue in two major states despite being legal in only seven — proof the "always in your pocket" shift is cannibalizing Vegas-style gambling and capturing younger, lonelier audiences. Regulators know the addiction risk is real; they just want the tax revenue more.
