Group C · The China side

BYD & Chinese EVs — walled out, so they build inside the walls

China's carmaker beat Tesla. The West slammed the door. BYD is climbing in through the window.

BYD makes cheap, genuinely good electric cars, and it wants to sell them everywhere. In 2025 it outsold Tesla to become the world's biggest EV maker. The West's response: build tariff walls to keep Chinese EVs out.

The walls went up

100%
US tariff on Chinese EVs — effectively a ban. The EU added tariffs up to ~45%. The message: not here.
2.26M
BYD's EV sales in 2025, beating Tesla's 1.6M — its first year on top, with over a million sold overseas.

The clever dodge

If you can't ship in, build inside

Instead of giving up, BYD is building factories inside the very countries trying to keep it out — Mexico, Brazil, Hungary, Thailand, Turkey. Make the car locally, and the tariff on imports doesn't apply. So the walls meant to block Chinese cars are pushing Chinese carmaking westward instead.

The main worry — it's at home, not abroad

The single biggest risk

Being shut out of the US hurts — but the real threat is in China: far too many EV factories (running at about half capacity) have set off a brutal price war. BYD's profit fell 19% and the industry burned tens of billions. The danger to BYD's margins is China's own overcapacity, not Western tariffs.

The surprising part

Tariffs meant to block Chinese EVs are accomplishing the opposite of what protectionists wanted: Chinese car factories on Western soil. The wall didn't stop BYD — it relocated it. And its biggest problem isn't the West at all; it's a savage price war back home.

In one sentence

BYD in a nutshell

It beat Tesla, got walled out of the West, and responded by building factories inside the walls — while its real fight is a brutal overcapacity price war at home.
