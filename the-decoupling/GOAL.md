# The Decoupling — Goal

Date baseline: 2026-08-06

## Objective

A plain-English collection: **how the US–China decoupling — tariffs, export controls, and reshoring — is reshaping company strategy.** Third instalment in the "strategy under a force" method, after `company-strategy-under-ai` (AI) and `ozempic-effect` (weight-loss drugs).

## The method (reused)

```
1. Pick one big force (here: the unwinding of cheap global trade — tariffs, US–China decoupling, reshoring).
2. Ask every company the same question: what did this force make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly valuable for you?
3. Group companies by the KIND of answer (archetypes).
4. Research each — plain English, verified facts kept separate from hype.
5. Pull out the patterns that repeat.
```

## Groups (archetypes)

- **A. Reshoring winners** — India, Mexico, the "electricians of reshoring," US chipmaking (CHIPS Act).
- **B. The exposed** — Apple, Nike & apparel, big-box retail.
- **C. The China side** — BYD & Chinese EVs, Shein & Temu, Alibaba / Chinese Big Tech.
- **D. Chokepoints & flows** — rare earths / critical minerals, shipping & logistics.
- **E. The money & the country** — the great decoupling as national strategy.
