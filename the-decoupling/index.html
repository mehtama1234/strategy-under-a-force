The Decoupling

How tariffs, export controls, and the US–China split are redrawing company strategy.

The third force, same method as the AI and Ozempic collections. For 30 years companies chased the cheapest place to make things — usually China. Now tariffs, export controls, and national-security fears are unwinding that. Everyone is being reshuffled, and each company here faces a different version of the shock.

The one question, asked of everyone

What did the great unwinding of cheap global trade make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly worth money for this company or country? The answer is different every time — and that difference is the story.
