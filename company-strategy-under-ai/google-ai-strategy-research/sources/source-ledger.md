## Known gaps / could-not-verify (do not overstate)

1. **Exact 2026 capex guide** — assembled from Q3-2025 "significant increase" language + sibling's $180-205B range; the precise Q2-2026-updated number is **[verify]**.
2. **Google Cloud Q2 2026 growth %** — the "~82%" from the Amazon dossier is **not** reconciled to Alphabet's release and is treated here as likely a basis error; use ~30-34% until verified. **Biggest single number to firm up.**
3. **AI Overviews / AI Mode monetization** — the "approximately the same rate" claim is **management assertion**, not an independently auditable disclosure. It is the crux and it is not externally verifiable in real time.
4. **Gemini exact frontier standing** — model version (2.5 vs 3) and benchmark leadership shift monthly; kept at [verify].
5. **Anthropic TPU deal $ / chip count** — "up to ~1M TPUs / tens of billions" is press+company framed; the drawn-down amount vs committed ceiling is not disclosed, and Anthropic explicitly runs multi-silicon (also Amazon Trainium, NVIDIA).
6. **TPU external revenue** — Google does not break out TPU-vs-NVIDIA or external-TPU revenue inside Cloud. The cost-advantage claim is structural/inferred, not a disclosed line.
7. **DOJ remedy final state** — appeals possible; the Sept 2025 ruling is the current baseline, not necessarily the end state.
8. **Power deals** — Kairos/CFS/Fervo are contracted/announced firm power for the **early-2030s**; they signal intent, not near-term (2026-2027) relief.
