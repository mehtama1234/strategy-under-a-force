# Watchlist: What Proves the Thesis, What Breaks It, What To Track (Google)

Date baseline: 2026-08-05

The thesis (see `reports/strategy-thesis.md`): Google is using the only complete, self-owned AI stack (Gemini + TPU + Cloud + DeepMind) to defend Search, scale a profitable TPU-differentiated Cloud, and distribute Gemini — fast enough that new AI profit outgrows any erosion of classic Search economics. This file is the falsification and tracking layer. The **single most important line is the Search-monetization race** (`reports/search-disruption.md`).

## 0. Scorecard as of Q2 2026 (what has actually materialized)

```text
PROVE-signals that have fired (bull):
  [x] Search still monetizing         -> Search ad revenue still GROWING while AI Overviews/AI Mode scale [verify]
  [x] Cloud profitable + growing       -> Cloud operating margin rising; growth ~30-34% off the 3rd-largest base [verify]
  [x] External TPU validation          -> Anthropic up to ~1M TPUs, >1 GW online 2026, "tens of billions" [G-T6]
  [x] Regulatory tail-risk eased        -> Sept 2025 DOJ remedy: NO Chrome/Android divestiture [G-P4]
  [~] FCF stays positive under capex    -> expected positive (vs Amazon negative) - the cash cushion holds [verify]

BREAK-signals to watch (bear):
  [ ] Search monetization rate leaks    -> NOT confirmed; the per-query RPM on AI surfaces is UNDISCLOSED (the crux)
  [ ] Query/attention defects to rivals -> NOT confirmed; ChatGPT/Claude pressure ongoing
  [ ] Gemini slips at the frontier      -> NOT confirmed; leadership shifts monthly vs GPT/Claude [verify]
  [~] Capex return anxiety              -> ~$180-205B 2026 guide invites scrutiny [verify]; still FCF-covered
  [ ] Cloud margin stalls               -> NOT confirmed; margin has been rising

Net: winning on TECHNOLOGY and (so far) HOLDING on Search; the decisive number (AI-Search monetization rate) is hidden.
```

## 1. What Would PROVE the Thesis

1. **Search holds while AI surfaces scale**: Search "& other" ad revenue keeps growing as AI Overviews/AI Mode take query share — evidence AI-native Search monetizes near the old rate. *The single most important signal.*
2. **Cloud revenue AND operating margin keep climbing**: the offset engine growing profitably, not just growing.
3. **External TPU adoption broadens beyond Anthropic**: named non-Anthropic labs/enterprises training/serving on TPU at scale — proof TPU is a product, not just internal + one anchor.
4. **Gemini holds frontier parity/leadership**: benchmark standing and usage competitive with GPT/Claude — the top of the stack stays valuable.
5. **Co-design efficiency shows up**: any disclosure/teardown showing Gemini-on-TPU cost per token below NVIDIA-based rivals.
6. **FCF stays positive through the capex ramp**: the cash cushion (the edge over Amazon) holds.
7. **Firm power energized on schedule**: Kairos/CFS/Fervo capacity actually coming online (early-2030s milestones on track).

## 2. What Would BREAK the Thesis

- **AI answers erode Search economics**: Search ad growth decelerates as AI usage rises, or management softens the "approximately the same rate" claim — cannibalization surfacing.
- **Query/attention defects**: users take "just answer me" behavior to ChatGPT/Claude faster than AI Mode/Gemini retains it; query volume or paid clicks visibly shift.
- **Gemini slips at the frontier**: falls behind on capability/trust, so the top layer of the stack loses value and owning the chip doesn't save it.
- **Cloud margin stalls**: TPU's cost edge fails to translate into durable operating margin; the intended offset underdelivers.
- **Capex outruns returns**: ~$180-205B [verify] converts to depreciation faster than Cloud/AI revenue ramps; margins compress; FCF turns negative.
- **Regulatory re-escalation**: the DOJ remedy is appealed/extended, or ad-tech/distribution constraints tighten, weakening the distribution funnel just as AI reshapes the product.

## 3. The Key Ratios To Track

```text
1. Search monetization:   Search "& other" ad revenue GROWTH RATE + any RPM-rate commentary (the crux)
2. Cloud as offset:       Google Cloud revenue growth, OPERATING MARGIN, and backlog/RPO
3. TPU externalization:   named external TPU customers beyond Anthropic; TPU-vs-NVIDIA mix (if ever disclosed)
4. Model competitiveness: Gemini frontier standing (benchmarks, usage/DAU) vs GPT/Claude
5. Co-design efficiency:  Gemini+TPU cost per token vs NVIDIA-based rivals (teardown/estimate)
6. Capital discipline:    capex vs depreciation vs Alphabet FCF and consolidated margin
7. Power conversion:      firm MW contracted vs energized (Kairos/CFS/Fervo) vs Cloud demand
```

## 4. Track Next Quarter (Concrete)

Each Alphabet earnings cycle, extract and update:

- [ ] Consolidated + segment revenue; **Search "& other" ad revenue and its YoY growth rate** (deceleration = the tell)
- [ ] Any management language on AI Overviews / AI Mode monetization rate (watch for softening from "approximately the same")
- [ ] Google Cloud revenue, growth %, **operating margin**, and backlog/RPO
- [ ] Total capex actual + updated full-year 2026 guidance; server (TPU vs NVIDIA) and data-center weighting
- [ ] Alphabet free cash flow and the capex/FCF relationship (does the cushion hold?)
- [ ] Gemini model releases, benchmark standing, and usage/DAU disclosures
- [ ] New/updated external TPU deals (beyond Anthropic); Anthropic TPU draw-down progress
- [ ] Power/nuclear/fusion status (Kairos, Commonwealth Fusion, Fervo) and any energization milestones
- [ ] DOJ remedy developments (appeals, distribution-deal changes)

## 5. Cross-Read Signals (Competitors)

- **NVIDIA** data-center revenue/margin — proxy for whether the chip bottleneck (and TPU's value) is easing or intensifying.
- **Amazon / Microsoft / Meta / Oracle** capex guidance — is the industry still escalating or beginning to digest?
- **Microsoft-OpenAI Azure AI** and **AWS Bedrock/Trainium** disclosures — the Cloud-demand competition Google Cloud must win.
- **OpenAI / ChatGPT usage** and any consumer-search moves — the direct answer-engine threat to Search that AI Mode counters.
- **Publisher referral-traffic data** — a proxy for whether AI answers are keeping clicks on Google's page (retention up, classic ad clicks down).
