## Core Question

Google owns the most complete AI stack — Gemini (own frontier model), TPU (mature custom silicon), Google Cloud (hyperscale, now profitable), and DeepMind (deepest research). On pure technology and supply, it is arguably the strongest-positioned hyperscaler. But it is the only one whose **existing core business is what AI most directly disrupts**: Search advertising. The question is whether Google can use its full-stack lead to make Search AI-native, scale Cloud + Gemini into new profit engines, and grow the whole even if classic Search economics erode.

## Early Finding

Where Amazon's risk is *capital* and Microsoft's is *margin*, Google's is **cannibalization** — the classic incumbent's dilemma of disrupting your own franchise before someone else does. Google's technology position is the best of the three; its business-model exposure is the worst. The dossier's job is to weigh those against each other.

## Current Strategic Thesis

```text
Full-stack AI leadership (Gemini + TPU + Cloud + DeepMind)
  -> defend Search by making it AI-native (AI Overviews, AI Mode)
  -> scale Google Cloud (profitable, TPU-cost-advantaged) into a second engine
  -> distribute Gemini across a billion-user surface
  = grow the sum even if classic Search is disrupted.
```

The bet pays off if AI-native Search monetizes near the old rate while Cloud + Gemini add enough profit to outgrow any Search erosion — and if TPU's cost advantage lets Cloud undercut NVIDIA-based rivals. It fails if AI answers erode Search economics faster than the new engines can replace them.
