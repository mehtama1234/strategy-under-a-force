# Watchlist: What Proves the Thesis, What Breaks It, What To Track (Cadence)

Date baseline: 2026-08-05

The thesis (see `reports/strategy-thesis.md`): Cadence is wrapping agentic workflows around owned engines (validators), grounded in JedAI data and powered by Millennium compute, to move — eventually — from time-based tool licenses toward outcome-linked pricing, while SD&A broadens the outcome domain. This file is the falsification and tracking layer. The **single most important signal is any move from license pricing toward usage/outcome pricing backed by repeatable closure.**

## 0. Scorecard as of 2026 (what has actually materialized)

```text
PROVE-signals partially fired (bull):
  [x] Durable recurring base            -> highly ratable ~$5.2B revenue, ~88% GM, large backlog [C-T1, verify]
  [x] Validator advantage is real       -> EDA's abundance of hard checks makes agentic closure credible here
  [x] Agentic products shipping          -> Cadence.AI (Cerebrus/Verisium/Allegro X/Virtuoso/Optimality) in market
  [x] Data + compute moat exists         -> JedAI (proprietary EDA data) + Millennium (GPU appliance, NVIDIA)
  [~] Loop-owning optimizers              -> Cerebrus (RL) + Optimality are credible loop-owners; others assistive [verify]
  [x] Outcome domain broadening           -> SD&A via organic + BETA CAE + OpenEye (vs Synopsys+Ansys)

PROVE-signals NOT yet fired (the gap):
  [ ] Repeatable MULTI-CUSTOMER closure  -> claims are vendor-sourced/demo-grade; independent proof thin
  [ ] Usage/metered revenue disclosed     -> NONE broken out separately from license
  [ ] Outcome/success-linked pricing      -> NONE disclosed - the value unit has NOT shifted
  [ ] Millennium as a systems line at scale -> unclear vs internal/demo accelerator [verify]

Net: a durable recurring franchise gaining pricing power + stickiness from agentic AI NOW;
     the bigger "tool -> outcome" repricing is credible but UNPROVEN. Phase 1, with optionality on Phases 2-4.
```

## 1. What Would PROVE the Thesis

1. **Usage/consumption revenue disclosed**: metered agentic-run or Millennium-compute revenue reported separately from license — the first hard sign the pricing model is shifting. *The single most important signal.*
2. **Outcome/success-linked contract language** in filings — the clearest "value unit shifted" evidence (Phase 4).
3. **Named, repeatable, baseline-normalized closure** across *multiple production customers*: PPA/coverage/debug/DRC/multiphysics — not demos or single reference cases.
4. **Demonstrated agent autonomy**: a Cadence.AI product shown owning a closure loop (iterate → validate → repair → escalate) on real designs, not just assisting.
5. **Millennium sold as systems** at scale — a hardware/appliance line distinct from software licenses.
6. **JedAI data-advantage evidence**: proof that proprietary EDA-run data *measurably* improves agent outcomes vs a generic model.
7. **SD&A outcome-domain traction**: multiphysics/structural/molecular (BETA CAE/OpenEye) closing validated *systems* outcomes — TAM broadening beyond chips.
8. **Compute economics net positive**: any disclosure that agentic-closure compute cost is below the engineer-time it replaces.

## 2. What Would BREAK the Thesis

- **Closure isn't repeatable**: demo-grade wins that don't generalize; the "10x / 20% PPA" [C-T4, claim] gains shrink or need heavy human babysitting on real industrial designs.
- **It stays a bundled feature**: Cadence.AI folded into existing licenses as an ordinary add-on; no usage/outcome pricing ever appears; the value unit never shifts.
- **Compute cost eats the productivity**: thousands of engine runs per closure cost more than the human saved — the loop doesn't pay.
- **Attribution/liability blocks outcome pricing**: fair baselines, outcome credit, and bad-design liability keep Phases 3-4 theoretical.
- **A competitor sets the standard first**: Synopsys+Ansys or Siemens proves repeatable closure / outcome pricing first and defines category economics.
- **"Agentic" proves mostly marketing**: autonomy claims outrun reality; the portfolio is assistive copilots relabeled — the industry-wide [verify] risk.

## 3. The Key Signals To Track

```text
1. Pricing model:      any DISCLOSED usage/metered/outcome revenue vs pure time-based license (the crux)
2. Closure evidence:   named, repeatable, baseline-normalized outcomes across MULTIPLE customers
3. Autonomy:           which products demonstrably OWN a loop vs assist (Cerebrus/Optimality vs Virtuoso/Verisium)
4. Compute economics:  cost per agentic closure vs engineer-time saved (does Millennium make it pay?)
5. Data moat (JedAI):  measurable evidence proprietary data improves outcomes
6. Outcome domain:     SD&A (BETA CAE/OpenEye/multiphysics) growth vs Synopsys+Ansys
7. Recurring health:   revenue growth, backlog, gross margin, ratable mix (the base funding it all)
```
