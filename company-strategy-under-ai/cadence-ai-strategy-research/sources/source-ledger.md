2. **Agentic vs assistive.** Cadence.AI products are marketed with increasing "agent" language, but the line between a **copilot/optimizer** (Cerebrus RL, Verisium triage) and a **true multi-step agent that owns a closure loop** is often blurred in marketing. Which products are genuinely agentic (own the loop) vs assistive is partly **[verify]**.
3. **ChipStack "40x"** — a separate startup's claim; must not appear in Cadence's evidence. The GOAL review checklist flags this explicitly.
4. **No usage/outcome-based pricing disclosed.** The revenue model remains time-based license/subscription/ratable; any metered-agentic-run or Millennium-compute revenue is **not** broken out. Outcome pricing is a *possible direction*, not a disclosed fact.
5. **Productivity/PPA claims are vendor-sourced.** Cerebrus "10x / 20% PPA", Millennium "80x" etc. are **[Disclosed claim]** — repeatable, baseline-normalized, multi-customer corroboration is the missing independent evidence.
6. **Millennium as a revenue line.** Whether Millennium is sold as a hardware/appliance line at scale (vs a demo/internal accelerator) is not separately disclosed. **[verify]**
7. **NVIDIA relationship depth** — partnership is real (compute + digital twins); any equity/strategic-investment specifics beyond product collaboration are **[verify]**.
