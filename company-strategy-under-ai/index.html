How AI is changing big companies

Not "do they use AI?" — but "what did AI make risky, or newly valuable, for their business?"

Each company here faces a different AI problem. That's the whole point of the collection — grouped below by the kind of problem. The four at the top are done in full depth; the rest are being filled in.

The one question, asked of everyone

What did AI make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly worth money for this company? The answer is different every time — and that difference is the story.
