Group A · Feeding the fur baby

Treats & supplements

The human wellness aisle — probiotics, calming gummies, CBD — now for dogs.

Once pets are family, they get the same wellness cabinet their owners do. Joint support, calming chews, probiotics, skin-and-coat oils, dental treats, CBD — the exact supplement stack humans buy, repackaged for dogs and cats. It's a high-margin impulse category that barely existed fifteen years ago.

The core bet

The bet is that a pet parent will spend on their dog's wellness the way they spend on their own — and grab a calming chew at the checkout the way they'd grab a magazine. Treats and supplements are cheap to make, easy to sell on emotion, and grow at double digits.

What the numbers say

~$150B
Total US pet spending in 2024. Treats and supplements are a fast-growing slice — an estimated $3–5B a year, rising 10–15% annually.
CBD boom
CBD pet products emerged around 2020 as the fastest-growing corner — now a multi-hundred-million category despite almost no regulatory oversight.
$50–150
What a single owner may spend per month on supplements alone — glucosamine, calming gummies, probiotics, omega oils — mirroring the human vitamin habit.
checkout
Treats and supplements moved from the pharmacy shelf to checkout and seasonal displays at Petco, PetSmart, and Amazon, driving bigger baskets like the human vitamin aisle.

Why it's such good business

These are impulse buys sold on love, not need. The margins are high, the purchase is emotional, and there's almost no proof required — a "calming chew" doesn't have to pass the trials a human drug would. That's the appeal and the risk in one.

What's newly valuable
Functional treats, calming and joint chews, pet CBD, and any product that lets an owner feel they're tending their pet's wellness.
What's risky
Claims are largely unverified. There's little standardization on quality or whether any of it works.
The single biggest worry

The whole category sits in a regulatory vacuum. CBD, probiotics, and "functional" chews for pets get far less oversight than human supplements — so if regulators or a lawsuit force real proof, the premium claims could collapse.

The surprising part

Pets now mirror the human wellness obsession almost exactly — the same joint pills, the same calming gummies, the same probiotics and omega oils. A category that didn't exist a generation ago is a multi-billion-dollar market built almost entirely on humanization and a parent's fear of not doing enough.

In one sentence

Treats and supplements turned a dog's wellness into a high-margin impulse aisle — the human vitamin habit, sold back to us for our pets, with almost none of the regulation.
