The Pet Economy

How treating pets as family — even as children — is reshaping company strategy.

The seventeenth force. Pets stopped being animals and became family members — "fur babies" with birthdays, insurance, and monthly food subscriptions. As people have fewer children, the money and love once meant for kids gets poured into pets, and every company that touches the animal treats it like a small, beloved, price-insensitive human. The one idea that ties it together: the pet became a child.

The one question, asked of everyone

What did pet humanization make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly worth money for this company? Once a pet was an animal you fed scraps and took to the vet only in a crisis. Now it's a family member with a wellness routine — and value flows to whoever premiumizes and medicalizes it.
