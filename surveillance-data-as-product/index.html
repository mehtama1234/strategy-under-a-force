Surveillance / Data as Product

How "you are the product" quietly reshapes what companies really sell.

The twentieth force. Many companies that look like they sell search, social, a car, a TV, or a credit card are really collecting data about you — and selling it, scoring you with it, or watching you with it. The thing you use is the bait; the profile is the product. The one idea: you are not the customer — you are the product.

The one question, asked of everyone

What did "your data is the product" make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly worth money? Value flows to whoever captures and refines the most data about you — an ad target, a credit score, a risk model, a face in a database. The risk is backlash: privacy laws, platform changes, and public revulsion, because the model runs on watching people who mostly never agreed to it.
