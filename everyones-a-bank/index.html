Everyone's a Bank

How the product became a front for the loan — and every company chased financial-services profit.

The twelfth force — sibling to the loyalty economy and the ad economy: same customer, same first-party data, but pointed at lending, payments, and float instead of ads. The one idea that ties it together: the product gets you in the door; the financing is where the money is made.

The one question, asked of everyone

What did financialization make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly worth money for this company? Selling a thing is low-margin and one-time. Lending against it, processing the payment, holding your money as "float," and insuring it are high-margin, recurring, and data-rich — so company after company turned its product into a front for a financial business.
