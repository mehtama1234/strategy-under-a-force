# Reshoring by Constraint — strategy under a force

The 35th collection (Tier-3, final) — Family I (macro forces). The aftermath/cost side of #3 the-decoupling.

**The force:** companies are being pushed to bring supply chains home (or to friendly countries) not because it's cheaper, but because they increasingly have no choice — tariffs, export controls, national-security rules, pandemic and shipping shocks, and big subsidies (CHIPS Act, IRA) make offshoring risky and reshoring mandatory. It's expensive, slow, short of workers, and still quietly dependent on China. We ask: *what did reshoring-by-constraint make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly valuable?*

**Signature insight (plain English):** we're not bringing factories home because it's cheaper — we're doing it because we're being forced to, and it's turning out to be hard. For 30 years the logic was pure efficiency: make it wherever it's cheapest. Now geopolitics, shocks, and policy have flipped the calculation to resilience and security, so companies reshore, near-shore, and friend-shore even at a higher cost. But the West forgot how to build factories: labor is short, the cost premium is real, the subsidies are enormous, and the finished "home-made" product still contains Chinese parts. Reshoring is a constraint, not a choice — and doing it well is the new hard problem.

**Groups:**
- **A — Why the tide turned:** the end of hyper-globalization (China+1) · the shocks that broke just-in-time (pandemic, Suez, war) · policy forcing it (tariffs, export controls, CHIPS/IRA)
- **B — Where it's going:** near-shoring to Mexico / friend-shoring to India & Vietnam · the chip & battery factory boom · the subsidy war (everyone bidding factories home)
- **C — The hard part:** the cost premium (it's more expensive) · the labor & skills shortage (who staffs the factories?) · the hidden dependency (you still need Chinese inputs)
- **D — The mechanism & reality check:** constraint not choice (compelled, not efficient) · is it real or reshoring theater? (announced vs built) · automation as the escape (robots make it viable)
- **E — The big picture:** reshoring-by-constraint (capstone) + patterns
