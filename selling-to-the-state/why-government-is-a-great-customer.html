Group D · The mechanism & the tensions

Why it's a great customer

Everyone mocks it as the worst customer. Statistically, it's the most reliable revenue on earth.

This is the mechanism. The government is the single largest buyer in the economy, it never goes bankrupt, its budgets are recession-proof (often counter-cyclical), and it almost never switches vendors. After decades of outsourcing plus a modernization, defense, and compliance surge, 'selling to the state' became a durable, attractive market despite the pain of the sale. Win once and you have the most predictable revenue in business.

The core bet

The government is huge, never dies, pays its bills, and almost never switches vendors — so once you win, you have the most durable revenue in business.

What the numbers say

~$6.5T
Annual federal spending; state and local add ~$4T — together 35–40% of all U.S. economic activity.
$700B+
Federal contract spending a year, split between defense and civilian agencies.
post-1980
When government outsourcing exploded, as agencies were pushed to 'do more with less' via contractors.
counter-cyclical
Government budgets often rise in recessions, when private customers cut — a hedge against downturns.
80–90%
Renewal rate for existing vendors — switching you out is so painful that agencies rarely try.

The most reliable revenue nobody respects

A $50M contract renewed every five years hits every payment on time and renews because Congress budgets for it. No startup can match that safety, which is why the painful, bureaucratic sale is actually one of the best deals in the economy — the horror stories hide how dependable the money is.

The single biggest worry

Political and budget risk is real — shutdowns, program cuts, and appropriations fights can freeze payments for weeks. And leaning on one giant customer is its own hazard: if that customer is a third of your revenue and Congress cuts the budget, the business can collapse.

The surprising part

The irony is that the customer everyone ridicules as slow and bureaucratic is statistically the most reliable on earth. Government pays late but always pays, renews almost automatically, and produces near-zero churn — a combination no private buyer offers, and the reason 'selling to the state' quietly became a premium business.

In one sentence

Government is the largest customer in the economy, pays reliably forever, almost never leaves, and spends even in recessions — which makes the painful sale one of the best deals in business.
