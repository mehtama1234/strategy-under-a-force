Group D · The mechanism & the tensions

The limits

The safest customer is also the most volatile — and the moat works both ways.

This is the skeptical piece. Depending on government has real downsides: political and budget risk (shutdowns, cost-cutting drives, program elimination), slow payment, reputational risk from controversial work, and the danger that reform erodes the very moats incumbents rely on. The same barriers that make the revenue durable also make you a hostage to politics — and the 2025–26 wave of shutdowns and cuts showed exactly how fast it can turn.

The core bet

The same things that make government a great customer — barriers, stickiness, big budgets — also create the risks: political whiplash, capture, and the chance the moat gets legislated away.

Your sponsor, not a competitor, is the real threat

What actually endangers a government contractor usually isn't a rival — it's the government itself changing the rules. Cost-cutting drives erase programs; certification automation lets startups certify in months instead of years; procurement-reform tools bypass the incumbents' playbook. The moat that kept competitors out can be drained by a single policy decision.

The single biggest worry

A single election or budget deal can vaporize an entire market overnight — foreign-aid and climate contractors saw exactly that in 2025–26 — and if automation and deregulation succeed, the barriers that protected incumbents disappear and pricing power goes with them.

The surprising part

The twist is that stability and volatility are the same coin. Government is safe because contracts are long and cancellation is rare — right up until a shutdown, a cost-cutting drive, or a reform makes it the opposite. Betting the whole company on one customer that reprices its priorities every four years is how a billion-dollar contractor becomes a target.

In one sentence

Government offers scale and stickiness, but a shutdown, a budget axe, or a reform that automates the barriers can turn the safest customer into the most dangerous one overnight.
