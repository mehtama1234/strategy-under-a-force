The synthesis · what repeats across all 13

The patterns

Line up every place cover is being withdrawn, and one theme dominates: when risk stops being poolable, the backstop walks away — and the cost cascades.

Each page asks: what did the insurance retreat make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly valuable? Thirteen answers later, the same tensions repeat.

The one thing to take away

Insurance quietly made risk invisible — a promise that if the worst happened, you'd be covered. That promise is being withdrawn wherever risk got too correlated, trending, or uncertain to pool. And because insurance is required to borrow, build, and drive, its retreat doesn't just raise prices — it strands assets and dumps the risk on individuals and the public.
