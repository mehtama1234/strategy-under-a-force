The Insurance Retreat

How the quiet backstop that lets modern life work is being withdrawn.

The 26th force. Insurance is the invisible backstop of modern life — you can't get a mortgage, run a business, drive, or build without it. Now insurers are pulling back: refusing whole regions and risk classes, raising prices, adding exclusions. When cover disappears, an asset becomes uninsurable, then unmortgageable, then unsellable. The one idea: the backstop is walking away.

The one question, asked of everyone

What did the insurance retreat make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly worth money? Value flows to whoever can price, transfer, or avoid the new risk (reinsurers, parametric insurers, self-insurers, risk-data firms). The danger is a cascade — uninsurable → unmortgageable → unsellable — that strands assets and dumps the risk on individuals and the public.
