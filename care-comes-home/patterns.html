The synthesis · what repeats across all 13

The patterns

Line up every corner of the shift home, and the same handful of mechanics repeat — all of them flowing from one flip: the money now rewards keeping people out of the hospital.

Each page asks: what did care-comes-home make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly valuable? Thirteen answers later, the same tensions repeat.

The one thing to take away

For a century, healthcare was built around the expensive building and everyone earned more the more you used it. Medicare Advantage and value-based care flipped that — now the provider profits by keeping you well and out of the hospital, so the hospital came home. It's cheaper and often better. But the savings are harvested by consolidators, the caring is done by underpaid aides and unpaid families, and the same flip that rewards prevention also rewards denial.
