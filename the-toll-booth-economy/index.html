The Toll-Booth Economy

How platforms that own a gateway tax everything — and everyone — that passes through.

The thirteenth force — from the discovery scout's queue. A platform sits in the middle of a market it controls and takes a cut ("take rate") of transactions it didn't originate. The one idea that ties it together: own the gateway, and you can tax the work everyone else does.

The one question, asked of everyone

What did the toll-booth economy make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly worth money for this company? The seller, driver, host, or creator does the actual work and takes the risk; the platform, sitting in the chokepoint, skims a cut of every transaction at near-zero cost — and the toll tends to ratchet up, because the captured supply can't leave.
