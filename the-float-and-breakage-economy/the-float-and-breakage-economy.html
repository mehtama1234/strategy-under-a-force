Group E · The big picture

Float & breakage

The biggest money sits still — in what companies hold before you spend it, and keep when you never do.

Pull the whole collection together and one line runs through it. A huge, quiet profit engine runs on money companies hold before you spend it (float) and money you never spend at all (breakage). They hold your Starbucks balance, invest it for free, and when you forget the last few dollars, it's pure profit at zero cost. It's doubly profitable — the money earns returns while it's in their hands, and a chunk never leaves — and it powers businesses that look nothing alike.

The core bet

Prepaid and held money is doubly profitable — you earn on it while holding it, and keep what's never claimed — so many 'product' companies are secretly float-and-forgetfulness businesses.

Winners and losers

The engine sorts cleanly into who holds your money first and who hands it over.

Who ends up ahead

Anyone who gets to hold your cash before you spend it — retailers, airlines, insurers, brokerages, payment and stablecoin firms, landlords, and subscription and gym companies — plus whoever keeps what you never claim.

Who's exposed

Customers who prepay and forget, watch their points quietly devalue, let gift cards expire, or earn nothing on held cash while the company earns 5% on it.

The single biggest worry

The model leans on high interest rates, loose regulation, and forgetful customers — all of which can reverse. Rate cuts shrink the float income, escheat and cash-sweep rules claw back breakage and force paying customers, and digital reminders make forgetting harder every year.

The surprising part

The note to end on is how universal the engine is once you can see it. The same hidden mechanism powers a coffee chain, an airline, a trillion-dollar insurer, a brokerage, and a crypto token — none of which look alike, all of which profit from money you give them that sits, earns, or vanishes. A dollar you prepay is worth more to them than a dollar you spend now, because they get to use it, keep part of it, and pay nothing if you never come back. The product on the sign is the costume; the real business is holding your money and quietly betting you won't claim it.

In one sentence

Companies profit from holding your money before you spend it — and profit even more when you never do.
