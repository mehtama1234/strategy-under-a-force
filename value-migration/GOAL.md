# Value Migration — strategy under a force

The 27th collection (Tier-2) — Family III (structural patterns).

**The force:** as a product or industry matures, the profit doesn't stay with whoever makes the thing — it migrates to whoever controls the new bottleneck (the distributor, the platform, the financer, the toolmaker, the aggregator). The maker gets commoditized; the money moves one layer over. We ask: *what did value migration make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly valuable?*

**The signature insight (plain English):** as a product matures, the profit flees the maker for the new bottleneck. A market starts with the product-maker earning fat margins. Over time, competition flattens the product into a commodity, and the margin migrates to a new layer — the distributor, the platform, the data owner, the financer, or the gatekeeper of demand. So the company that "wins" isn't the one that makes the best product; it's the one that ends up owning the layer where profit pools next. Value flows to whoever controls the scarce, un-commoditized step; the risk is that you can be a brilliant maker and still watch all your profit walk next door.

**How it differs from toll-booth / locked-in:** those describe one company's deliberate playbook. Value migration is about the profit flow shifting as the whole market evolves — competition plus time, not a single company's trap.

**Groups:**
- **A — The profit leaves the maker:** pharma → PBMs/pharmacy · hardware → software (Wintel) · cars → financing & the dealer
- **B — Disintermediation → re-intermediation:** music labels → streaming (Spotify) · publishers → platforms (Amazon, TikTok) · retailers → marketplaces (brands → Amazon)
- **C — The new bottlenecks:** chips → foundry & design tools (TSMC/ASML/EDA) · the AI stack (who captures AI's profit) · the "smiling curve" (value at the ends, not the middle)
- **D — The mechanism & the moves:** conservation of attractive profits (Christensen/Slywotzky) · integrate or be commoditized (vertical capture) · spotting the next migration
- **E — The big picture:** value-migration (capstone) + patterns
