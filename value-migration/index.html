Value Migration

How the profit quietly flees the company that makes the thing — and pools at the new bottleneck next door.

The 27th force. A market opens with the product-maker earning fat margins. Then competition turns the product into a commodity, and the profit walks off — to the middleman, the platform, the financer, the toolmaker, the aggregator. The company that "wins" isn't the one that makes the best product; it's the one that ends up owning the layer where the money pools next. The one idea: as a product matures, the profit flees the maker for the new bottleneck.

The one question, asked of everyone

What did value migration make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly worth money? Making the thing became cheap and interchangeable; owning the scarce next layer — access, distribution, the standard, the tool, the loan — became where all the profit lives. The danger: you can be a brilliant maker and still watch every dollar of profit walk next door.
