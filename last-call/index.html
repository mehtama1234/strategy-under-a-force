Last Call

How the decline of drinking is quietly reshaping company strategy.

The sixteenth force — from the discovery scout's queue. A younger, health-conscious generation is drinking less, and an economy built on the assumption that adults drink is scrambling. The one idea that ties it together: the default drink stopped being alcohol.

The one question, asked of everyone

What did the decline of drinking make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly worth money for this company? For a century "let's grab a drink" meant booze. Now — helped by cannabis, by GLP-1 drugs that mute cravings, and by science reframing alcohol from "healthy" to "carcinogen" — value flows to whoever serves the sober or moderate drinker.
