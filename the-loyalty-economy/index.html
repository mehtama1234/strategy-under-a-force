The Loyalty Economy

How the customer relationship — the app, the points, the data — became worth more than the product.

The tenth force, same method as the nine before it — and it grew out of a McDonald's annual report. For a century, a company sold a product and the sale was the end of the story. Now the sale is just the doorway. The one idea that ties it together: the product is the bait; the relationship is the business.

The one question, asked of everyone

What did the loyalty economy make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly worth money for this company? Once it enrolls you — app, points, membership, a stored balance — it monetizes the relationship many ways: frequency, first-party data (priceless after tracking cookies died), retail-media ads, prepaid float, even selling your points to a bank. Again and again, that's worth more than the product.
