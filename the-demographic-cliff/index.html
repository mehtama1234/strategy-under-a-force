The Demographic Cliff

How an aging, shrinking population quietly reshapes company strategy.

The sixth force, same method as the AI, Ozempic, Decoupling, Cheap Money, and Energy Transition collections. Birth rates fell below replacement across most of the world, and the giant post-war generations are retiring. So the population is aging fast — and in many countries, shrinking. Here's the one idea that ties it all together: for 200 years there were always more people coming. That just stopped.

The one question, asked of everyone

What did the demographic cliff make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly worth money for this company or country? More people every year was the hidden escalator under almost every business. Value now flows to whoever serves the old and the scarce — and drains from whoever needed the crowd to keep growing.
