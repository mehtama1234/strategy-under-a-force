The Climate Bill

How a hotter, wilder planet reshapes company strategy — and who ends up paying.

The seventh force, same method as the six before it. This one is the physical side of climate change — not the clean-energy build-out (that was the Energy Transition collection, about preventing it), but living with the heat, floods, and fires already locked in. The one idea that ties it together: a stable climate was a hidden subsidy nobody noticed — and the bill is now coming due, priced first by insurers.

The one question, asked of everyone

What did a hotter planet make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly worth money for this company? Weather used to be a free, stable backdrop. Now it's a growing cost someone must price and pay. Value flows to whoever can price the risk, defend against it, or move to safety — and drains from whoever sits in harm's way assuming the old climate.
