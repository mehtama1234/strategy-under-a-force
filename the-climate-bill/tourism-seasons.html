Group D · The big shifts

Tourism & seasons — the map of where you ski and grow wine is moving

Warming is literally relocating the zones where you can ski, grow grapes, or comfortably vacation.

A hotter planet is redrawing the map of leisure. The zones where you can reliably ski, grow wine grapes, or take a summer holiday are shifting toward the poles and up the mountains — creating new winners in places that were never supposed to compete, and hollowing out old strongholds.

The map on the move

  • Skiing: low-altitude resorts are losing reliable snow — closing early or leaning on expensive artificial snow. Some Colorado resorts closed weeks early (2026); one didn't open for the first time in 20+ years.
  • Wine: Bordeaux's output fell to its lowest since 1991 (2024) under heat and mildew — while England and Scandinavia now grow serious wine. The map is moving north.
  • Mediterranean summers: brutal heat and wildfires (Greece hit 41°C for over two weeks, 2023) are denting peak-season travel to places where tourism is ~20% of GDP.
84% of reefs
Share of the world's coral reefs hit by the worst-on-record bleaching (2023–25) — fueling "last-chance tourism" as people rush to see reefs and glaciers before they're gone.

The main worry

The single biggest risk

Low-altitude ski towns and Mediterranean destinations face economic collapse as their core season becomes unreliable or dangerous — hollowing out whole local economies that were built entirely around a season the climate no longer guarantees.

The surprising part

The losers aren't losing to bigger rivals — they're losing to places that were never supposed to compete. A town in Yorkshire, England, can now rival Bordeaux for wine. And there's a bleak irony in "last-chance tourism": the crowds rushing to see glaciers and coral reefs before they vanish are, by flying there, hastening the vanishing. Meanwhile the cost of propping up old seasons (snowmaking, frost management) keeps rising, making them harder to defend against the newly-viable regions where the climate simply works now.

In one sentence

Tourism & seasons in a nutshell

Warming is relocating the map of where you can ski, grow wine, and vacation — lifting cool, northern, high places while shrinking the seasons of old strongholds into a few weeks nobody wants.
