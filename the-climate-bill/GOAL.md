# The Climate Bill — strategy under a force

The seventh "strategy under a force" collection (after AI, Ozempic, the US–China decoupling, the end of cheap money, the energy transition, and the demographic cliff).

**The force:** a hotter, wilder planet — more heat, floods, fires, droughts, and storms — and the growing cost of living with it. IMPORTANT: this is the *physical-impact and adaptation* side of climate. The energy-transition collection was about **preventing** climate change (clean-energy supply). This one is about **living with the change already locked in** — the damage, the risk, and who pays. We ask of each company or sector: *what did a hotter planet make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly valuable?*

**The signature insight (plain English):** for all of civilization, a stable climate was a free backdrop you could take for granted — build a house, plant a crop, or set an insurance premium assuming next year's weather looks like the last century's. That stability was a giant **hidden subsidy nobody named**. A hotter, wilder planet removes it: weather turns from a stable background into a growing, uncertain **cost that someone has to price and pay**. Value flows to whoever can **price the risk** (insurers, reinsurers, risk modelers), **defend against it** (cooling, water, resilience), or **move to safety** — and drains from whoever sits in harm's way still assuming the old climate. And **insurance is the canary**: it's the first system forced to put a real number on the risk, so it's where the future shows up first. When a place becomes uninsurable, it quietly becomes unmortgageable, unsellable — and the climate hits its value long before the flood does.

**Groups:**
- **A — The risk-pricers (the front line):** property insurers · reinsurers & cat bonds · climate-risk data & modeling
- **B — The physically exposed:** coastal & floodplain real estate · farming & food · utilities & wildfire
- **C — The adaptation winners:** air conditioning & cooling · water & scarcity · resilience & construction
- **D — The big shifts:** climate migration & "safe" havens · carbon markets & liability (who pays) · tourism & shifting seasons
- **E — The big picture:** the-climate-bill (capstone) + patterns

**Method / constraints:**
- Research on Haiku, done directly by each agent (NO sub-agents), facts separated from hype.
- Plain, everyday language. No jargon, no clichés. Short sentences, real numbers with dates, concrete examples.
- Self-contained dark-theme HTML pages sharing styles.css, grouped hub + patterns synthesis, cross-linked to the six sibling collections.
