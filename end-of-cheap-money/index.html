The End of Cheap Money

How the jump from near-zero interest rates to much higher ones reshaped company strategy.

The fourth force, same method as the AI, Ozempic, and Decoupling collections. For about 15 years money was almost free to borrow. Around 2022 that ended — rates jumped to ~5% — and suddenly capital had a cost again. That one change quietly reshuffled who wins and who loses across the whole economy.

The one question, asked of everyone

What did the end of cheap money make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly worth money for this company? Warren Buffett's line is the key: interest rates work like gravity on everything — and gravity came back.
