# The End of Cheap Money — Goal

Date baseline: 2026-08-06

## Objective

A plain-English collection: **how the end of near-zero interest rates — the shift to much higher rates from 2022 on — reshaped company strategy.** Fourth in the "strategy under a force" method, after AI (`company-strategy-under-ai`), weight-loss drugs (`ozempic-effect`), and the US–China split (`the-decoupling`).

## The method (reused)

```
1. Pick one big force (here: money going from free to expensive).
2. Ask every company the same question: what did higher rates make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly valuable for you?
3. Group companies by the KIND of answer (archetypes).
4. Research each — plain English, verified facts kept separate from hype.
5. Pull out the patterns that repeat.
```

## Groups (archetypes)

- **A. Cash is king again** — big banks, insurers & Berkshire, savers & money-market funds.
- **B. Built on free money** — money-losing tech, startups & VC, SPACs & crypto.
- **C. The debt bombs** — commercial real estate, regional banks (SVB), private equity, zombie companies.
- **D. The government & housing** — the US interest bill, housing & mortgages.
- **E. The big picture** — the end of cheap money (the regime shift).

## Method note

Research run on **Haiku**, with agents told **not to spawn sub-agents** (keeps it fully on Haiku — no Opus leak, as validated on the Decoupling collection). Pages written in plain-English house style; verified facts separated from hype; note that by 2026 rates may be easing, so frame it as the *era* shift and its aftermath.
