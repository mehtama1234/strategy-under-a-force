PEOs & HR outsourcing

A small firm hands its payroll, benefits, and compliance to a co-employer — and never touches an I-9 again.

A PEO — professional employer organization, like ADP TotalSource, Insperity, TriNet, Justworks, Rippling, or Gusto — becomes the legal "co-employer" of a small company's staff, running payroll, benefits, compliance, and workers' comp so the small business doesn't have to. By pooling thousands of small firms together, PEOs get big-company benefit rates and absorb the HR complexity that now crushes solo operations.

The core bet

Small firms trade a slice of payroll for the promise of never doing HR alone again. Pooling many small employers lets the PEO buy Fortune-500-grade benefits and shoulder a legal-compliance burden that has become impossible for a 50-person company to track.

Leverage through pooling

The real win isn't hands-off ease — owners still spend time interfacing with the PEO and battling for exceptions to cookie-cutter policies. It's professional management at commodity cost: you get the playbook Fortune-500 firms use for ~$30/employee/month instead of a $60k general counsel. It's not magic — it's leverage through pooling.

Who ends up ahead

PEOs (recurring % of payroll) and the small firms that get enterprise benefits and legal armor cheaply.

Who's exposed

Small businesses that lose HR control, and workers if the PEO cuts corners on claims to keep its rates low.

The single biggest worry

Incentives can misalign: a PEO earns on payroll percentage and workers' comp, so it has a mild pull to keep benefits lean or deny claims to suppress its rates — while the small business still eats the legal and reputational fallout of any compliance miss. And if the PEO fails, payroll and records can vanish mid-cycle.

The surprising part

The counterintuitive part is that PEOs don't cut the HR workload as much as the marketing claims — owners still interface constantly. What they really sell is access to institutional-grade HR at a small-business price, converting a fixed, expensive function (a real HR department) into a variable per-head fee.

In one sentence

PEOs solved the compliance crisis that killed small-firm HR — pooling many employers to deliver big-company benefits and legal cover for a few percent of payroll.
