The Energy Transition

How the shift from burning fuel to running on electricity reshaped company strategy.

The fifth force, same method as the AI, Ozempic, Decoupling, and Cheap Money collections. The world is slowly swapping oil, gas, and coal for electricity made from sun, wind, and batteries. Here's the one idea that ties it all together: energy is turning from a fuel you rent forever into a machine you build once.

The one question, asked of everyone

What did the energy transition make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly worth money for this company? Old world: value flows to whoever sells the fuel again tomorrow. New world: it flows to whoever builds the machines and mines the materials.
