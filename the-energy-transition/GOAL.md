# The Energy Transition — strategy under a force

The fifth "strategy under a force" collection (after AI, Ozempic, the US–China decoupling, and the end of cheap money).

**The force:** the world is shifting from burning fossil fuels to running on electricity made from renewables (solar, wind, batteries). We ask of each company or sector: *what did the energy transition make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly valuable?*

**The signature insight (plain English):** energy is turning from *a fuel you rent forever* into *a machine you build once*. You used to buy oil, gas, and coal over and over, every day, forever. Now you build a solar panel or a battery once and the fuel — sunshine, wind — is free. That single shift reshuffles everything: it destroys the "sell fuel again tomorrow" business, rewards the people who **build the machines** and **mine the materials**, and slowly makes electricity abundant and cheap. Whoever owns the machines and the minerals wins; whoever sells the fuel loses.

**Groups:**
- **A — The picks-and-shovels (they sell to everyone):** electric utilities & the grid · copper & critical minerals · grid equipment (transformers)
- **B — The disrupted incumbents:** oil & gas majors · gas stations · legacy automakers
- **C — The pure-plays / believers:** solar & wind · EV makers · batteries & storage
- **D — The petrostates & geopolitics:** the Gulf petrostates · China's clean-tech grip
- **E — The big picture:** the-energy-transition (capstone) + patterns

**Method / constraints:**
- Research on Haiku, done directly by each agent (NO sub-agents), facts separated from hype.
- Plain, everyday language. No jargon, no clichés. Short sentences, real numbers with dates, concrete examples.
- Self-contained dark-theme HTML pages sharing styles.css, one page per topic, grouped hub + patterns synthesis, cross-linked to the four sibling collections.
