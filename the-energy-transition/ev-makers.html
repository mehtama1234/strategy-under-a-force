Group C · The believers

EV makers — cheap and huge beat cool and premium

For years Tesla was "the future." Then the transition rewarded something more boring: scale and low cost.

The pure electric-car makers thought the prize would go to whoever had the coolest, most premium EV. Instead the transition rewarded whoever could build a cheap, good car in enormous volume — and that turned out to be China's BYD, not Tesla. The companies that assumed EVs would stay premium got wiped out.

The order flipped

  • BYD overtook Tesla. It passed Tesla in pure-EV sales (first in a quarter back in 2023, then for the full year 2025) by winning on price — cheap models like the Seagull — not on flashy tech.
  • A price war crushed margins. Starting January 2023, Tesla cut prices hard and everyone followed. Tesla's gross margin fell from ~30% (2021) to ~18% (2024); profit per car dropped by half.
  • The startups burned out. Fisker went bankrupt (2024). Rivian lost tens of thousands of dollars per vehicle; Lucid lost ~$500,000 per car. Billions raised, billions gone.

BYD's battery cost, well below rivals — because it makes ~60% of its own batteries (cheap LFP chemistry). That vertical integration is the whole moat.

The main worry

The single biggest risk

The only survivors are those who can make money at brutal, sometimes negative margins at 1M+ cars a year — the BYD model. For Tesla, endless price wars eat away the "we're special" valuation. For everyone smaller, it's simply existential: get costs down to BYD's level, or die.

The surprising part

Tesla was once the world's most valuable carmaker partly because investors priced in an EV monopoly — the belief that electric cars would stay premium and Tesla would own them. The reality: EVs became a commodity in about three years, and the market tipped to the lowest-cost maker. The transition didn't crown Tesla the future — it turned Tesla into a regular automaker competing on price and scale, where BYD's cheap batteries and giant supply chain give it the edge.

In one sentence

EV makers in a nutshell

The transition made electric cars a commodity far faster than anyone expected, so the prize went to the cheapest high-volume maker (BYD) — not the coolest premium one (Tesla) — and punished every startup that bet EV premiums would last.
