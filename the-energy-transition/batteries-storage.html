Group C · The believers

Batteries & storage — the master-key that makes solar work at night

Solar is cheap but useless at midnight. The battery fixes that — and whoever owns batteries owns the transition.

Solar power is dirt cheap but worthless after sunset. The battery is the fix: it soaks up cheap daytime power and hands it back at night, turning on-and-off renewables into round-the-clock electricity. Battery costs collapsed, grid storage is booming — and that makes the battery the single most important machine in the whole transition.

The cost collapse changed everything

−93%
Fall in battery pack prices, 2010 to 2024 — from ~$1,436 to ~$115 per kWh. In 2024 they dipped below $100/kWh for the first time.

The grid-storage boom

  • California went from ~12 GW of storage to ~16 GW in under a year (2024–25) — batteries now routinely cover the evening peak as the sun goes down.
  • Texas added ~3.9 GW in 2024 alone and is on track to overtake California. The US grid added ~11 GW in 2024, heading to ~18 GW in 2025 — near-exponential growth.
  • The point: storage makes solar "dispatchable" — available when you actually want it, not just when the sun happens to shine.

The main worry

The single biggest risk — China owns the chain

Two Chinese companies, CATL (~38%) and BYD (~17%), make over half the world's batteries. China also controls the raw materials, the cell-making, and the winning chemistry. If geopolitics breaks that supply line, the transition stalls — and the West has no domestic alternative at scale. (This links straight to the China clean-tech page.)

The surprising part

The battery industry quietly stopped chasing the "best" battery. For years the race was about energy density — squeezing more range into each pound. Then the winner became a cheaper, older chemistry: LFP (lithium-iron-phosphate), pioneered in China. It gives up some range but wins on cost, safety, and lifespan — and by 2025 it powered 80%+ of Chinese EVs. The moat flipped from performance to price, which is exactly the game China plays best.

In one sentence

Batteries & storage in a nutshell

The battery is the master-key that turns cheap-but-intermittent solar into round-the-clock power, but because two Chinese firms make over half of them, the transition's most important machine is also its biggest dependence.
