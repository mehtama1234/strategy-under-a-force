Group A · Your health, rolled up

Physician practices — bought as billing machines, not care

PE bought the parts of medicine that behave like a repeatable retail business — and optimized the billing.

Private equity rolled up the profitable, repeatable corners of medicine: dermatology, eye care, gastroenterology, orthopedics, fertility, and ER staffing. After acquisition, the data shows more visits, more procedures billed, and more care shifted to cheaper non-doctor staff. The practices were bought less as care providers than as billing machines.

The evidence

+4.7–17%
Higher procedure volume at PE-acquired dermatology practices vs independents, at higher prices — with more biopsies billed and more work shifted to physician assistants (24.7% of Medicare skin biopsies by 2019, up from 19.5% in 2017).
  • Envision Healthcare (KKR-owned) filed for bankruptcy (May 2023) after the No Surprises Act killed its "surprise billing" model — EBITDA collapsed from ~$1B to $250M.
  • TeamHealth (Blackstone) filed 80,000+ out-of-network billing disputes in six months of 2023; PE firms spent $55M+ lobbying to defend surprise billing.

The main worry

The single biggest risk

The buyout optimizes for revenue extraction, not patient need — more procedures, cheaper staff, aggressive billing. And the debt that made the deal attractive becomes fatal when the billing engine is regulated away (Envision).

The surprising part

Envision's collapse is the smoking gun. It lost $750M of annual profit in two years once a law made its core trick — out-of-network "surprise" billing — illegal. That reveals the real play: PE didn't buy medical practices to deliver better care; it bought them as billing machines, then lobbied to protect the billing. When the lobbying failed, the leverage that made the deal attractive turned lethal.

In one sentence

Physician practices in a nutshell

PE bought the repeatable, high-margin corners of medicine as billing machines — optimizing for procedure volume, cheaper staff, and aggressive billing — until regulation killed the trick and the debt sank the chain.
