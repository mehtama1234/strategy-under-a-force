The Roll-Up

How private equity quietly bought Main Street — one boring, essential local business at a time.

The fourteenth force — from the discovery scout's queue. Private equity takes a fragmented industry of small local businesses, buys them one by one, bundles them into a big chain (keeping the local names so you don't notice), raises prices, loads on debt, and sells it on. The one idea: buy the boring, raise the prices, financialize.

The one question, asked of everyone

What did the roll-up make scarce, cheap, safe, risky, or newly worth money for this business? The profit comes not from running it better but from financial engineering and pricing power over customers who don't realize their friendly local vet, dentist, or funeral home now answers to a fund in New York.
